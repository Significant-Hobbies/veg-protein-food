## Design Specificity Verdict

The result is strongly authored for Mint Index. Cobalt rules, acid-lime source
actions, tabular nutrition values, reconciliation totals, suppressed ratios,
and in-row evidence sheets express a specific food-research instrument rather
than generic dashboard chrome. The main drift is compositional: the identity
moment behaves like a marketing hero, delaying the dense operating surface the
direction promised.

The deterministic scan returned zero findings for `index.html`. Browser proof
at 1440 and 390 pixels found no horizontal overflow, no console errors, and a
working mobile evidence disclosure. Live detector overlay injection was skipped
because the available browser evaluation surface is read-only; screenshots,
DOM geometry, disclosure state, and console logs provided the fallback signal.

## Overall Impression

Credible, distinctive, and unusually honest about estimate quality. The single
biggest opportunity is moving users from orientation to recipe comparison
sooner, especially on phones.

## What's Working

- The visual system is specific and consistent across desktop and mobile.
- Trust is a product mechanic: Creator, Estimated, Suppressed, coverage,
  unmatched lines, and provenance are visible rather than buried.
- Inline evidence is the strongest interaction and preserves context.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Essential mobile labels and actions are undersized.** Metric labels
   reach 8–9px and Evidence/Watch actions are 38px high. Raise mobile labels to
   11px and primary interaction targets to at least 44px.
2. **[P1] The operating surface arrives late.** The opening hero and five-cell
   reconciliation strip delay the first recipe, particularly at 390px. Compress
   the mobile opening and preserve the distinctive hierarchy with less height.
3. **[P2] Filters become expensive deep in 263 rows.** A compact sticky return
   affordance or collapsed secondary-filter pattern would reduce travel.
4. **[P2] Watch is more prominent than evidence.** Estimated and partial rows
   benefit from equal or stronger evidence emphasis before source handoff.
5. **[P2] Snapshot failure offers no Retry control.** Add a direct local retry
   action while retaining the plain-language diagnosis.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Power user:** repeated comparison passes require returning to a non-sticky
  control deck from deep in the ledger.
- **Accessibility-dependent user:** semantics and focus are strong, but the
  smallest labels and action heights undercut low-vision and motor access.
- **Distracted mobile user:** the first recipe follows a long orientation and
  full control stack, making the initial path feel slower than the data model.

## Minor Observations

- The word Snapshot disappears on phones, leaving a bare date.
- The evidence panel is resolved enough to carry more decision-making weight.
- The empty-state reset and focus return are particularly good.

## Questions to Consider

- Is the protagonist the count of recipes, or finding the right recipe?
- If evidence is required to trust an estimate, should Watch remain the only
  acid-lime action?
- Could mobile reach its first result by the second viewport without losing the
  reconciliation story?
