# Recipe Index

Recipe Index is a curated, source-linked comparison of 50 egg-free vegetarian
recipes. Every included recipe publishes at least 10 g protein per serving and
at least 10 g protein per 100 calories for the same serving.

## What you can do

- Search by recipe, ingredient, or original publisher.
- Exclude ingredients you do not want to use.
- Filter by protein source, format, dietary fit, and practicality in India.
- Sort by protein, calories, or protein per 100 calories.
- Open the original recipe before cooking it.

## Evidence boundary

Nutrition values come from each recipe publisher and remain tied to the
publisher's serving basis. Recipe Index does not recalculate missing quantities,
and substitutions can change the result. The collection is a comparison aid,
not medical or dietary advice.

The default collection contains 45 foods and five drinks from 14 publishers.
It is intentionally curated rather than exhaustive.
