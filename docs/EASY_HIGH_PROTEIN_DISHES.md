# Easy high-protein cooking playbook

This is a practical reference for the dishes discussed while building Recipe
Index. It explains how each dish works and links to a complete recipe. It does
not promote every linked recipe into the dashboard.

## What counts as verified

- **Admitted** means the exact recipe publishes at least 10 g protein per
  serving and at least 10 g protein per 100 kcal, using protein and calories
  reported for the same serving.
- **Related admitted base** means the dashboard contains a qualifying recipe
  built around the same protein source, but not this exact preparation.
- **Technique reference** means the source is useful for learning the dish, but
  its nutrition is missing, incomplete, or below the dashboard threshold.

Protein density is calculated as `protein ÷ calories × 100`. A protein-rich
ingredient does not guarantee a qualifying dish: oil, cream, cheese, wraps,
rice, and toppings can quickly dilute the final ratio.

## The dishes

### 1. Soya keema masala

**Why it works:** Soya granules behave like minced meat after hydration and
absorb Indian masala well. They are inexpensive, shelf-stable, and widely
available in India.

**Basic method:** Hydrate the granules, drain and squeeze them thoroughly, then
cook them in an onion-tomato masala with ginger, garlic, peas, and spices. Let
the mixture cook down until it is moist rather than watery.

**Useful references:** [Dassana's soya keema](https://www.vegrecipesofindia.com/soya-keema/)
and [Swasthi's soya keema](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/soya-keema/)
both document the Indian technique. The dashboard's
[Minced Soya Chunks](https://yellowchilis.com/high-protein-vegan-minced-meat/)
is the related admitted example.

**Keep it dense:** Measure the oil, use peas as a supporting ingredient rather
than the bulk of the dish, and treat roti or rice as a separately portioned
side. The exact keema references are technique references until their
same-serving macros qualify.

### 2. Tofu bhurji

**Why it works:** Crumbled firm tofu cooks quickly and takes on the familiar
flavor structure of paneer bhurji while generally offering a better
protein-to-calorie ratio than full-fat paneer.

**Basic method:** Drain firm tofu and crumble it. Cook onion, chilli, tomato,
turmeric, cumin, and the remaining spices; add the tofu near the end so it
warms through without becoming dry.

**Useful reference:** [Swasthi's tofu bhurji](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/tofu-bhurji/)
documents a pantry-friendly Indian version. The dashboard's
[Easy Tofu Scramble](https://www.thevegangym.com/the-mediocre-vegan-kitchen-recipes/easy-tofu-scramble)
is the related admitted example.

**Keep it dense:** Use firm or extra-firm tofu, keep oil modest, and add bread
or potatoes deliberately rather than treating them as part of the measured
recipe.

### 3. Seitan tikka

**Why it works:** Prepared seitan is mostly wheat protein, has a chewy bite,
and stands up to yogurt, ginger, garlic, chilli, and tandoori spices.

**Basic method:** Cut cooked seitan into bite-size pieces, marinate it in a
thick yogurt-spice mixture, then grill, air-fry, or cook it in a hot pan until
the edges brown. Serve it dry as tikka if protein density is the priority.

**Useful references:** [My Dainty Soul Curry's seitan tikka masala](https://www.mydaintysoulcurry.com/vegan-seitan-tikka-masala-healthy-recipe/)
and the [Seitan Society's version](https://seitansociety.com/recipes/vegan-seitan-chicken-tikka-masala/)
show the marinating and cooking technique. The dashboard's
[Quick Seitan Two Ways](https://cookingforpeanuts.com/how-to-make-seitan/)
is the related admitted base recipe.

**Keep it dense:** Tikka and tikka masala are not nutritionally interchangeable.
A rich sauce can erase seitan's advantage. For example, this
[seitan tikka masala](https://kuhnrikon.com/us/recipes/vegan-tikka-masala-with-seitan.html)
publishes 29 g protein at 356 kcal, or 8.1 g per 100 kcal, so it does not pass
the dashboard gate.

### 4. Chilli tofu or seitan

**Why it works:** The Indo-Chinese chilli, garlic, soy sauce, capsicum, and
spring-onion treatment gives either tofu or seitan a strong flavor without
requiring a creamy sauce.

**Basic method:** Brown unbattered tofu or prepared seitan, stir-fry the
aromatics and capsicum, then toss everything in a small amount of thickened
chilli-soy sauce.

**Useful reference:** [Holy Cow Vegan's chilli tofu](https://holycowvegan.net/chilli-tofu/)
documents the tofu preparation. Replacing tofu with seitan is a cooking option,
not a verified nutrition substitution.

**Keep it dense:** Skip deep frying and heavy batter, limit sugar and oil, and
measure any rice separately. This dish remains a technique reference until an
exact version publishes qualifying macros.

### 5. Low-fat paneer tikka

**Why it works:** Paneer takes marinades and high heat exceptionally well, and
a genuinely low-fat paneer can improve the protein-to-calorie ratio.

**Basic method:** Marinate paneer and vegetables in thick yogurt, ginger,
garlic, lemon, and tikka spices. Thread or spread them in one layer, then grill
or air-fry just until browned.

**Keep it delicious:** Low-fat paneer can become rubbery, so use a thick
marinade and avoid overcooking it.

**Evidence status:** Technique reference only. “Low-fat” on its own is not
enough evidence; a future dashboard entry needs a complete recipe with protein
and calories published for the same serving.

### 6. TVP taco or wrap filling

**Why it works:** TVP is fast, shelf-stable, and absorbs smoky or spicy
seasoning. The filling also works in Indian-style wraps with onion, coriander,
and mint chutney.

**Basic method:** Hydrate TVP with seasoned hot water, drain if necessary, then
brown it with onion, tomato, chilli, cumin, paprika, or taco seasoning. Add
beans only if their calories still fit the intended ratio.

**Useful references:** [Oops Vegan's TVP taco filling](https://www.oopsvegan.com/en/blog/vegan-taco-filling)
and [My Quiet Kitchen's TVP tacos](https://myquietkitchen.com/tvp-tacos/)
document straightforward versions.

**Keep it dense:** Evaluate the assembled meal, not merely the filling. Large
tortillas, cheese, avocado, sour cream, and oily sauces can dominate its
calories. This remains a technique reference until the exact assembled serving
has qualifying published macros.

### 7. Chocolate protein mousse

**Why it works:** Thick Greek yogurt provides the body, while protein powder
raises the protein without needing cream. Cocoa and a small amount of fruit or
sweetener make it taste like dessert.

**Basic method:** Blend or whisk the yogurt, protein powder, cocoa, and the
source recipe's flavoring ingredients until smooth, then chill it.

**Admitted source:** [The Protein Chef's low-calorie protein mousse](https://theproteinchef.co/low-calorie-protein-mousse-recipe/)
publishes 34.5 g protein at 259 kcal per serving. Recipe Index excludes the
optional pie-crust treatment from its ingredient summary.

**Keep it dense:** Treat nut butter, granola, chocolate chips, and crust as
extra ingredients whose calories require a new calculation.

### 8. Vegan protein pancakes

**Why it works:** Vegan protein powder and a small amount of flour or oats make
an egg-free batter with much more protein than standard pancakes.

**Basic method:** Mix the dry ingredients, stir in the wet ingredients only
until combined, rest briefly, then cook small pancakes on a non-stick pan.

**Admitted source:** [The Vegan Gym's blueberry vegan protein pancakes](https://www.thevegangym.com/the-mediocre-vegan-kitchen-recipes/vegan-blueberry-protein-pancakes)
publish 13 g protein at 118 kcal per serving. [Romy London's vegan protein pancakes](https://www.romylondonuk.com/basic-vegan-protein-pancakes-recipe/)
are an additional egg-free technique reference, not a separately admitted
dashboard entry.

**Keep it dense:** Syrup, oil, chocolate, and nut butter count. If they are
added, reassess the complete plated serving rather than retaining the batter's
ratio.

## Handling the core ingredients

- **TVP and soya granules:** Hydrate only until tender, squeeze out excess
  water, and brown after squeezing so the texture is not spongy.
- **Tofu:** Firm or extra-firm tofu is the easiest choice for bhurji and
  stir-fries. Drain it well; pressing is optional when the recipe will cook off
  moisture.
- **Prepared seitan:** It is already cooked. Brown or grill it briefly instead
  of simmering it for a long time, which can make it rubbery.
- **Protein powder:** Add it gradually and preserve enough moisture. Excess
  powder can make pancakes chalky and mousse pasty.
- **Low-fat paneer:** Marinate well and stop cooking once it browns; prolonged
  heat makes lean paneer tough.

## Standard for documenting another dish

Before adding a future dish to Recipe Index, record:

1. A direct link to the original complete recipe.
2. Whether the complete dish is egg-free and vegetarian.
3. Its principal protein source and key ingredients.
4. The publisher's serving basis.
5. Protein and calories published for that same serving.
6. The resulting protein per 100 kcal.
7. Whether the ingredients are easy to obtain in India.
8. Any optional toppings or substitutions excluded from the published macros.

If items 4 or 5 are missing, keep the dish as a technique reference rather
than estimating it into the checked collection.
