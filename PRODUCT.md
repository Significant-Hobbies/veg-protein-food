# Product

## Platform

Web.

## Users

People looking for egg-free vegetarian dishes that deliver useful protein for
their calories. The primary operator wants recipes practical with Indian
ingredients, needs to remove recipes containing unwanted ingredients, and
wants a direct original source before relying on a recipe.

## Product purpose

Recipe Index is a curated, source-linked comparison tool. Its homepage collects
recipes whose publisher-supplied serving macros clear two explicit gates: at
least 10 g total protein and at least 10 g protein per 100 kcal. It favors
actual food over shakes and makes ingredient practicality visible.

## Scope

In scope:

- Egg-free vegan and vegetarian recipes with source-published calories and
  protein for the same serving.
- A 10 g total-protein and 10 g-per-100-kcal admission gate.
- Direct credit and original links for every publisher or YouTube creator.
- Key source ingredients, serving basis, and macro caveats.
- India-fit labels: easy, adaptable, or specialty.
- Recipe/publisher/ingredient search, removable ingredient exclusions,
  protein-source, format, India-fit and dietary filters, and sortable nutrition
  columns.
- Food-first default ordering with drinks explicitly categorized and limited to
  five of the 50 recipes.

Out of scope:

- Medical or dietary advice.
- Recalculating a publisher's nutrition panel from ingredient quantities.
- Guaranteeing that substitutions preserve the published macros.
- Automatic source refresh, accounts, persistence, or deployment.

## Brand commitments

- Standalone product: no Fleet navigation, terminology, styling, or runtime
  dependency.
- Curated usefulness over exhaustive scraping.
- Every nutrition value is labelled as publisher-supplied and tied to a serving
  basis; approximate source values stay approximate.
- Soy/TVP, tofu, tempeh, seitan, whey, vegan powder, dairy foods, and legumes
  are all valid protein strategies when the source evidence clears both gates.
- Shakes remain discoverable but never dominate the default collection.

## Accessibility

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, preserve complete keyboard access, use text with every state
color, support reduced motion, and structurally reflow the ledger on phones.
